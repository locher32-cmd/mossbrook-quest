MossbrookQuest

A board-game adventure for up to five heroes on one computer or tablet. Take turns tapping the die, walking the map and choosing what to do. Every adventure is a brand-new land with its own map, weather, boss and side quest. Math is your power!

Tap a hero to join, then pick their riddle level. Gentle mode gives two big answers and lets you try again, and Read aloud speaks the riddles. Everything works with a mouse or a finger. Pass the tablet to whoever has the turn.

How to play

  • Your turn: tap the die. The roll plus 2 is how many squares you can walk.
  • Tap any glowing square to walk there. Drag the map to look around.
  • Tap a tree, rock, berry bush, chest, the bridge or the crafting table to walk over and use it. Using something ends your turn.
  • Tap Elder Bramble to talk. It's free and does not end your turn.
  • Sparkles on the ground are surprise squares.
  • After every hero moves, the slimes take their turn. Tap a slime near you to start a battle.
  • Battles: every hero can Attack, cast a Big Attack or a Heal (solve the riddle to make it work), share a Berry, Defend or Run.
  • Watch out: Blue slimes splash everyone, and Spiky slimes hurt you back when you hit them.
  • The campfire refills everyone's hearts. The crafting table sharpens swords for 5 wood and 5 stone.
